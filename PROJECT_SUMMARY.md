### Color Palette
```css

- Coral: #F76250, #FA7268, #F77565
- Teal: #05ABA9, #9CE5DE

Secondary Colors:
- Teal Dark: #047C7A, #0E7C79
- Teal Light: #7BD7D6
- Coral Soft: #F98E7E, #FF8A73
```

### Typography
- Font Family: System fonts (Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif)
- Headings: Bold, clear hierarchy
- Body: 1.125rem (18px) for readability
- Line Height: 1.6-1.8 for accessibility

- SM: 1rem (16px)
- MD: 2rem (32px)
- LG: 3rem (48px)
- XL: 4rem (64px)
- XXL: 6rem (96px)

- Buttons (primary, secondary, outline variants)
- Forms with validation
- Statistics with animations
- Testimonials
- Grid layouts (2, 3, 4 columns)
