Key Outcomes

💼

Employment Success

85% of our graduates secure employment within 6 months of completing their course. Many find jobs in their desired field, often with our partner organizations.

📈

Career Advancement

60% of employed learners report career progression within 12 months, including promotions, increased responsibilities, or transitions to better positions.

💰

Income Growth

Graduates report an average income increase of 35% after completing our programs and securing new employment or advancing in their careers.

🎓

Course Completion

We maintain a 92% course completion rate, significantly higher than industry averages, thanks to our supportive learning environment.

🌟

Confidence Building

98% of learners report increased confidence in their language or digital skills, empowering them to pursue opportunities they previously thought impossible.

🤝

Community Integration

Our programs help learners integrate into their communities, build professional networks, and participate more fully in society.

Impact Across Communities

Supporting Immigrants & Refugees

Over 40% of our learners are immigrants or refugees rebuilding their lives in the UK. We provide the language and digital skills they need to integrate, find employment, and contribute to their communities.

Empowering Women

55% of our learners are women, many returning to work after career breaks. We provide flexible learning options and supportive environments that enable women to balance education with family responsibilities.

Bridging the Digital Divide

We help older adults and those from disadvantaged backgrounds develop digital literacy, ensuring they're not left behind in an increasingly digital world.

Creating Economic Opportunity

Our programs directly contribute to economic mobility, helping individuals move from low-wage jobs to skilled positions with better pay and career prospects.

Building Confidence

Beyond technical skills, we help learners build confidence, self-esteem, and belief in their abilities - transformations that extend far beyond the classroom.

Strengthening Families

When adults gain education and employment, entire families benefit. Our impact extends to children, partners, and communities, creating ripple effects of positive change.
