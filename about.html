About Learning for Change

Building bridges to opportunity through education

Our Story

Learning for Change was founded on a simple but powerful belief: that everyone deserves access to quality education, regardless of their background or circumstances. We recognized that in an increasingly digital and globalized world, language barriers and lack of digital skills were preventing talented, motivated individuals from reaching their full potential.

What started as a small community initiative has grown into a comprehensive educational organization serving adults across the UK and beyond. We've helped thousands of learners gain the English language proficiency and digital competencies they need to thrive in modern workplaces and society.

Today, we work with a diverse range of partners in hospitality, retail, technology, and other sectors to ensure our courses are aligned with real industry needs. Our learners don't just gain qualifications—they gain the confidence and practical skills to transform their lives.

Our Vision & Mission

🌟

Our Vision

A world where language and digital competencies are not barriers to participation, employment, or social contribution. We envision a future where every adult, regardless of their starting point, has access to the education and support they need to build a better life for themselves and their families.

We see a society where diversity is celebrated, where skills gaps are addressed proactively, and where education is recognized as a fundamental right and a pathway to opportunity.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide inclusive and accessible education in English and Digital Skills for adults in the UK and globally. We are committed to:

  • Delivering high-quality, practical education
  • Removing barriers to learning
  • Supporting learners throughout their journey
  • Partnering with employers to ensure relevance
  • Creating pathways to employment and advancement

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Who We Serve

Understanding our learner community

Age Profile

Our learners span a wide age range, reflecting the diverse needs of adult education:

  • 18-25 years: Young adults entering the workforce or seeking career advancement
  • 26-40 years: Professionals looking to upskill or change careers
  • 41-55 years: Experienced workers adapting to digital transformation
  • 55+ years: Mature learners pursuing new opportunities or staying connected

Learner Backgrounds

We work with a diverse community including:

  • Recent immigrants and refugees
  • Career changers and job seekers
  • Working professionals seeking advancement
  • Parents returning to the workforce
  • Individuals with limited formal education
  • People from underserved communities

What Makes Us Different

👥

Adult-Focused Approach

Our programs are specifically designed for adult learners, recognizing their unique needs, experiences, and learning styles. We respect the knowledge and skills our learners bring.

💼

Industry Partnerships

We work closely with employers to ensure our courses teach the skills that are actually needed in the workplace. Many of our learners transition directly into employment with our partners.

🔄

Flexible Learning

We understand that adult learners have complex lives. Our flexible scheduling and multiple learning formats make it possible to study while working or caring for family.

🎓

Qualified Instructors

Our teachers are not only qualified educators but also experienced professionals who bring real-world insights to the classroom.

🌐

Holistic Support

We provide more than just classes. Our learners receive career guidance, mentoring, and ongoing support to help them succeed beyond the classroom.

📊

Proven Results

Our track record speaks for itself. High completion rates, strong employment outcomes, and satisfied learners demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach.
