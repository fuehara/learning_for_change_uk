Volunteer Opportunities

Use your skills to empower others

Teaching & Tutoring

Support learners in developing English or digital skills. We welcome qualified teachers as well as professionals who can share their expertise.

Requirements:

  • Teaching qualification or relevant expertise
  • Patience and empathy for adult learners
  • Commitment of 3-4 hours per week
  • DBS check (we can arrange)

Conversation Partners

Help English learners practice their speaking skills in a relaxed, informal setting. No teaching qualification required - just patience and good communication skills.

Requirements:

  • Native or fluent English speaker
  • Good listening and communication skills
  • 2 hours per week commitment
  • Friendly and encouraging attitude

Career Mentoring

Guide learners as they navigate career transitions or job searches. Share your professional experience and help them achieve their employment goals.

Requirements:

  • Professional work experience
  • Mentoring or coaching skills
  • 2-3 hours per month
  • Understanding of UK job market

Administrative Support

Help with administrative tasks, event organization, social media, or other behind-the-scenes work that keeps our programs running smoothly.

Requirements:

  • Organizational skills
  • Computer literacy
  • Flexible hours available
  • Reliable and detail-oriented

IT & Digital Support

Assist learners with technical issues, help maintain our digital infrastructure, or support online learning platforms.

Requirements:

  • IT or digital skills
  • Problem-solving abilities
  • Patient teaching approach
  • Flexible availability

Event Volunteers

Help with open days, graduation ceremonies, fundraising events, and community outreach activities. Great for those with limited time availability.

Requirements:

  • Enthusiastic and friendly
  • Good people skills
  • Occasional availability
  • Team player attitude

How Donations Are Used

Direct Program Support (70%)

The majority of donations go directly to program delivery - paying qualified instructors, purchasing learning materials, and maintaining our learning facilities and technology.

Learner Support Services (20%)

Funding for career guidance, mentoring, childcare support, travel assistance, and other services that help learners succeed in their studies.

Program Development (7%)

Investment in new courses, curriculum updates, and innovative teaching methods to ensure we're meeting evolving learner and employer needs.

Administration (3%)

Essential operational costs to keep the organization running efficiently and ensure we can continue serving our community.
