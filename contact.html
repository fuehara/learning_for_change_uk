Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

You can register your interest through our contact form or by calling us directly. We'll guide you through the enrollment process and help you choose the right course for your needs.

Are the courses free?

Many of our courses are free or heavily subsidized for eligible learners. We also offer payment plans for fee-based courses. Contact us to discuss your situation and available options.

What are the class schedules?

We offer flexible scheduling with morning, evening, and weekend classes to accommodate working adults. Specific schedules vary by course. Contact us for current timetables.

Do you offer online courses?

Yes! Many of our courses are available both in-person and online. We use modern learning platforms to ensure online learners receive the same quality education as in-person students.

How can my company partner with you?

We offer various partnership models including recruitment partnerships, customized training, and strategic collaborations. Visit our Partnerships page or contact us to discuss options.

Can I volunteer if I don't have teaching experience?

Absolutely! We have volunteer opportunities for all skill levels, from conversation partners to administrative support. We provide training and support for all volunteers.
