Partner with Us

Build a skilled workforce through strategic education partnerships

Why Partner with Learning for Change?

We bridge the gap between education and employment, creating win-win solutions for organizations and learners.

In today's competitive market, finding skilled talent with the right language and digital competencies can be challenging. Learning for Change partners with organizations to develop customized training programs that prepare learners for success in your specific industry.

Our partnerships create a pipeline of qualified, motivated candidates who are ready to contribute from day one. We handle the training, you gain access to talented individuals who understand your industry's needs.

How Partnership Works

1

Consultation

We meet to understand your organization's needs, challenges, and goals. Together, we identify the skills and competencies you're looking for.

2

Customization

We design or adapt our training programs to align with your specific requirements, incorporating industry-relevant content and scenarios.

3

Training Delivery

Our expert instructors deliver high-quality training to learners, preparing them with both technical skills and soft skills for your industry.

4

Placement Support

We connect you with qualified graduates, facilitate interviews, and provide ongoing support to ensure successful placement and integration.

Partnership Models

Flexible options to suit your organization

Recruitment Partnership

Access our pool of trained candidates for your hiring needs. We provide pre-screened, qualified candidates who have completed relevant training programs.

Best for: Organizations with regular hiring needs

Customized Training

We develop and deliver bespoke training programs specifically for your organization, either for new hires or existing employees looking to upskill.

Best for: Organizations with specific training requirements

Strategic Partnership

Long-term collaboration including co-designed programs, work placements, mentoring opportunities, and ongoing talent pipeline development.

Best for: Organizations committed to long-term workforce development
